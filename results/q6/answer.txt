Directed functional connectivity was estimated from simultaneously recorded single-unit activity over
the full recordings of 18 mice. Within each mouse, I matched the three areas to the unit count of
the smallest recorded area, drew five random sets of matched units, and summed their spikes into
population counts.

The primary analysis used a regularized Poisson GLM with 10 ms bins and 100 ms of recent history.
For each direction, I compared a full model with a model omitting the source area's history. Both
models included the target's own history, the third area's history, and a slow target activity
term. I used three contiguous time blocks for cross-validation and expressed the held-out
log-likelihood gain in bits per target spike. I averaged the five draws within each mouse and
used the mouse as the independent observation.

Mean gains were 0.00251 for area 1 => 2 (95% CI 0.00106 to 0.00396), 0.00034 for area 3 => 2
(-0.00003 to 0.00071), and 0.00028 for area 3 => 1 (0.00001 to 0.00055). Those numbers are reported 
in the table above. Area 1 => 2 was above
zero (Holm-adjusted p = 0.00592). In paired mouse-level comparisons, it exceeded area 3 => 2 by
0.00217 (Tukey-adjusted 95% CI 0.00027 to 0.00406, p = 0.0239) and area 3 => 1 by 0.00222
(0.00042 to 0.00403, p = 0.0151). Area 1 => 2 ranked first at all five bin-width and history
settings tested.

Jitter-corrected cross-correlograms pointed in the same direction. With 5 ms lag bins and 200 ms
jitter windows, area 1 => 2 peaked at +10 ms and had a mean positive-minus-negative asymmetry
over mirrored 5-50 ms lags of 0.00209 normalized CCG units (95% CI 0.00032 to 0.00386,
Holm-adjusted p = 0.0462). The asymmetry exceeded area 3 => 2 (Tukey-adjusted p = 0.0028), while
the comparison with area 3 => 1 was inconclusive (p = 0.102). 

Conditional transfer entropy was
inconclusive because none of the candidate directions gave a positive mean held-out gain. It is 
tricky to use for the spiking data (see https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008054).

Based on the primary Poisson GLM, brain area 1 => brain area 2 had the strongest directed
functional connectivity among the three candidates.
