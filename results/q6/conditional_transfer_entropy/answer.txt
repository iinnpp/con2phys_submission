Q6 answer: Not enough data / no differences

Full-recording population spike counts from 18 mice, with 5 matched unit draws per mouse.
Primary conditional transfer entropy setting: 10 ms bins and 100 ms short history.
The score is the held-out gain in categorical log probability from adding source history,
conditional on the target and third-area short histories and the slow target state.
The primary comparison uses bits per original target spike. The score in bits per bin is also saved.
The target response uses counts 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5+; histories use training-fold count tertiles.
Negative estimates can arise when the estimated full distribution predicts worse on held-out bins.
The mouse is the independent unit. Direction mean confidence intervals are marginal 95% t intervals.
The three paired direction comparisons use Tukey-adjusted p-values and simultaneous 95% intervals.
Their standard errors come from within-mouse differences. Tukey adjustment is approximate when these standard errors differ.
The three tests against zero form a separate family with Holm-adjusted p-values.
A direction is selected if it is above zero and exceeds both alternatives at adjusted p < 0.05.
